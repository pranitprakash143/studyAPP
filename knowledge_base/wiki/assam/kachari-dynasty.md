KACHARI DYNASTY

Claimed descent from Ghatotkach. Known as Mech, Dimasa, Bodo or Bodo-fisa.

Contemporary to Sukapha was Bicharpatipha.

Defeated by Suhungmung in 1526 AD (called thapita sancita).

Revolted in 1531, defeated. In 1536, king Detsung beheaded by Suhungmung.

Shifted capital to Maibang on Mahur river.

Conquered by Koches in 1562 (Kamalnarayan appointed governor. Koch settlers known as Dehans).

Satrudaman (Jasa Narayan) invaded Jaintia (Dhana Manik), took title Asimardan and Pratap Narayan. Changed capital to Kirtipur.
