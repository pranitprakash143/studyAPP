EARLY RULERS

Suteupha (1268-1281), Subinpha (1281-1293), Sukhangpha (1293-1332) - marriage with Kamata ruler Pratapdhvaj's daughter Rajani.

Sukhrangpha (1332-1364), Sutupha (1369-1376) - murdered by Chutiyas. Tyaokhamti.

SUDANGPHA / BAMUNI KONWAR (1397-1407)

First official entry of Hinduism to Ahom court.

Worship of Vishnu continued with Ahom deity Chom-Cheng (Chomdeo).

Shifted capital to Charagua near Dihing.

First to perform coronation ceremony 'Singari-ghar-utha' (paatghar, holonghar, singarighar). Custom of beheading a man.

Followed by: Sujangpha, Suphakpha, Susenpha, Suhenpha, Supimpha.

SUHUNGMUNG (1497-1539)

Known as 'Dihingia Roja' (shifted capital to Bakata).

Assumed Hindu title 'Swarganarayan', adopted 'Saka' era.

New Minister post: 'Barpatra Gohain' in 1504 (Konseng).

First census. Dihingia, Chringia, Tungkhungia, Tipamiya faids.

Annexed Chutiya 1523. 'Phra-Sen-Mong' First Sadiyakhowa Gohain.

Rikkhvan ceremony.

Defeated Kacharis 1527 & 1536 (beheaded Detchung). New officer 'Marangikhuwa Gohain'.

In 1532, Turbak Khan invaded Assam (Moriyas/Goriyas).

SUKLENMUNG (1539-1553)

Gorhgayan Raja (shifted capital to Gorgaon).

Placed Chom-Cheng outside palace.

First Ahom ruler to issue coin.

Mula Gabharu Day 29 May. Sati Sadhani Divas 21 April.

SUKHAMPHA (1553-1603)

Khura Raja.

Conflict with Koch (1562 attack by Chilarai). Treaty of Majuli 1563.

Durga puja celebrated.

1576 Nara King of Mungkang failed expansion.

SUSHENGPHA / PRATAP SINGHA (1603-1641)

Burha Raja, Budhi Swarganarayan, Gajapati (owner of thousand elephants).

New posts: Barbarua (Momai Tamuli/Sukoti) - head of east of Koliabor. Barphukan (Langi Panisia) - head of west of Kaliabor.

Assamese official language.

Posa system - Tribal appeasement.
