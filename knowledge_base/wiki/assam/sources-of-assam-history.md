SOURCES OF ASSAM HISTORY

Buranji.

Persian chronicle: Tabaqat-i-Nasiri by Minhaj-ul-Siraj, Fathiya-i-Ibriyya (aka Tarikh-i-Assam) by Shihabuddin Talish.

Foreigners' account: Account of Ralph Fitch, 'Geographical sketch of Assam' by John Peter Wade (accompanied Welsh).

Haygriva Madhav Temple

Stone temple enshrining image of Hayagriva Madhava.

Kalika Purana talks about origin and establishment in Monikut hill.

Constructed by King Raghudeva Narayan in 1583 (or Pala Dynasty in 10th century according to some).

Some Buddhists believe Buddha attained Nirvana here.

Continuous row of elephants carved on lowest level - akin to stone cut temple of Ellora.
