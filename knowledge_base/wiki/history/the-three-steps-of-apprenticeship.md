### The Three Steps of Apprenticeship

1. **Deep Observation (The Passive Mode):** Muting one's ego to observe the rules and power relationships of the new environment.
2. **Skills Acquisition (The Practice Mode):** Engaging in the "tedium" of practice to hardwire knowledge into the nervous system, often cited as requiring roughly 10,000 hours.
3. **Experimentation (The Active Mode):** A gradual process of self-assertion and testing one's own ideas.

> "The skill to mold the material into what we want must be learned and attentively cultivated." — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe
