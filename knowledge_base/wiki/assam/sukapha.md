SUKAPHA

Born 1191, left homeland 1215 AD.

Reached Brahmaputra valley (Namrup) in 1228 (Assam Divas 2nd December).

Mungdunsunkham (country full of golden gardens).

First tribes to submit - Barahi (Thakumtha) and Maran (Bdaucha).

Established first capital in Charaideo in 1253.

Assisted by Buragohain and Borgohain.

Deodhai and Bailungs asked to write Buranji. Died in 1268.
