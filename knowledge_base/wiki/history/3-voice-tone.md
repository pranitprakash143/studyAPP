3. Voice & Tone
How VisionFlow speaks to the audience.
The Persona: Imagine a knowledgeable friend who is one step ahead on the journey. You are
not a lecturer; you are a guide.
Attribute We Are... We Are Not...
Tone Encouraging, Objective, ClearPreachy, Hype-driven, Condescending
LanguageAccessible, Concise, Active Jargon-heavy, Passive, Verbose
Feeling "I can do this / I understand this""This is too complicated for me"
Export to Sheets
The "VisionFlow" Writing Rules:
Accent Color: Clarity White
Hex: #FFFFFF or Off-White #F8F9FA
Usage: Backgrounds for blog posts, main text.
Alert/Warmth Color: Sunrise Orange
Hex: #FF9F1C
Usage: Call to Actions (CTAs), Subscribe buttons, Urgent news highlights.
Headings (YouTube Thumbnails & Blog Titles): Montserrat or Poppins (Bold/Extra Bold).
Why: Geometric, modern, and highly readable on small mobile screens.
Body Text (Blog & Captions): Open Sans or Inter (Regular).
Why: Neutral and easy on the eyes for long-form reading.
Symbol: An abstract "V" that looks like a forward arrow or a flowing wave.
Wordmark: "Vision" in bold/strong weight, "Flow" in a lighter/thinner weight to denote
movement.
1. Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF): In both videos and blogs, state the value proposition within
the first 30 seconds or first paragraph.
