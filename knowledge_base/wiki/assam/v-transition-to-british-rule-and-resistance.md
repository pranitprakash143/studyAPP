## V. Transition to British Rule and Resistance

* **Treaty of Yandabo (Feb 24, 1826):** Ended the First Anglo-Burmese War; Burma renounced all claims to Assam, leading to British annexation.
* **Revolts:**
* **Gomdhar Konwar (1828):** First revolt against British rule.
* **1857 Revolt:** Led by **Maniram Dewan** (hanged 1858) and Piyali Barua.
* **Phulaguri Uprising (1861):** First agrarian revolt (against poppy cultivation ban).
