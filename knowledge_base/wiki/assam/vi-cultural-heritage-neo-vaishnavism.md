## VI. Cultural Heritage: Neo-Vaishnavism

* **Srimanta Sankardeva (1449–1568):** Propagated **Eksaran Namdharma** (monotheistic devotion to Krishna).
* **Literary Works:** Sankardeva wrote the *Kirtan Ghosa* and *Gunamala*; **Madhavdeva** wrote the *Nam Ghosa*.
* **Institutions:** **Satra** (monastery) and **Namghar** (prayer hall) became the pillars of Assamese social and cultural life.
* **Art Forms:** Introduced **Borgeet**, **Ankiya Nat** (plays), and the **Sattriya** dance.
