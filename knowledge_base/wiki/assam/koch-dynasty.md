KOCH DYNASTY

Progenitor Haria Mandal (Chikanagram, Goalpara).

Son Bisu (Biswa Singha) founded Kingdom in 1527 AD. Died in 1540.

Naranarayan (Original name - Malladev). Commander in chief - Sukladhvaj, Sangram Singha, Chilarai.

Defeated Ahoms in 1563 (Treaty of Majuli).

Established authority over Jaintia, Tripura, Sylhet. Conquered NE India within 4 years (1562-65).

Rebuilt Kamakhya temple with bricks in 1565.

Chilarai died 1572-75 of smallpox.

Kingdom divided between Naranarayan and Chilarai's son Raghudeva. Naranarayan died 1587.

Naranarayan known as Vikramaditya of Assam. Court scholars: Sankaradeva, Madhavdev, Prurshottam Vidyabagish, Ram Saraswati, Ananta Kandali, Sridhar, Bakul Kayastha.

Raghudeva died 1603 -> Parikshit Narayan (capital North Guwahati). Parikshit surrendered to Lakshminarayan & Mughals. Brother Bali Narayan took refuge with Ahoms.
