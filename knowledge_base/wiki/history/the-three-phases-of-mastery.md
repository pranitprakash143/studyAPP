## The Three Phases of Mastery

The journey toward mastery consists of three distinct evolutionary stages:

* **The Apprenticeship:** An initial phase of deep observation and skill acquisition where the individual learns the basic elements and rules of their field.
* **The Creative-Active:** A second transformation where the individual begins to experiment, play with internalized rules, and make new associations between different ideas.
* **Mastery:** The final stage where knowledge, experience, and focus are so deep that the individual can see the "whole picture" with complete clarity and act with high-level intuition.
