Rulers: Niladhvaj -> Chakradhvaj -> Nilambar.

Alauddin Hussain Shah (1498)

Bengal Sultan Alauddin Hussain Shah led a campaign.

Territory upto Hajo annexed. First success of Muslims in mediaeval Assam.

Sultan set up his son Daniel as ruler of Kamata. Bhuyans destroyed Daniel's garrison.

Ghiyasuddin Aulia built a mosque at Hajo, considered Poa-Mecca (one-fourth of Mecca).
