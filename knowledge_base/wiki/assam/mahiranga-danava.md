MAHIRANGA DANAVA

The earliest known king of ancient Assam. Kirata chief.

Capital was at Mairanka (Identified with the Mairang Parvat near Guwahati).

Succeeded by Hatakasura, Sambarasura, Ratnasura, and Ghatakasura.

Ghatakasura was killed by Naraka.
