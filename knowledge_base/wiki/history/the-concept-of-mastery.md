## The Concept of Mastery
### Definition and Characteristics
- **Mastery** is defined as a form of **power** and **high-level intelligence**.
- It represents the **peak of human potential**.
- It is characterized by a "feeling that we have a greater command of **reality**, **other people**, and **ourselves**".

### Attainment of Mastery
- This state is **not** the result of:
  - **Natural talent**
  - "**Magic**"
- It is achieved through a **rigorous process**.
- It is accessible to anyone who:
  - Follows their **innate inclinations**.
  - Submits to a **specific developmental path**.
