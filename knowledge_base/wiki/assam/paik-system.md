PAIK SYSTEM

AHOM - MUGHAL CONFLICT

Battle of Samdhara (1616)

First battle between Mughals and Ahoms. Spies under Ratan Shah killed. Qasim sent Syed Aba Bakr. Ahoms got info from Ahkek Gohain. Mughals slaughtered.

Treaty of Asuror Ali (1639)

Barnadi on north bank, Asurar Ali on south fixed as boundary. Signed by Allah Yar Khan and Momai Tamuli Barbarua.

SURAMPHA / BHAGA RAJA (1641-44) & SUTYINPHA / NARIYA ROJA (1644-48)

JAYADHWAJ SINGHA (1648-1663)

Ahom name Sutamla. Disciple of Niranjandev (Auniati sattra). First king to formally accept Vaishnava tenets. First copper-plate land grants.

War of succession 1658 (Aurangzeb). Mir Jumla invaded 1662 (accompanied by Shihabuddin Talish - Fathiya-i-Ibriaya). Captured Garhgaon. Jayadhwaj fled (Bhagania Roja).

Treaty of Ghilazharighat (1663): Negotiated by Dilir Khan & Atan Burhagohain. War indemnity, 20 elephants. Ramoni Gabharu sent to Mughal harem (Rahmat Banu Begum).

CHAKRADHWAJ SINGHA (1663-1670)

Supungmung. Appointed Lachit Deka as Barphukan. Recaptured Gauhati 1667.

Raja Ram Singh (with Guru Tegh Bahadur) sent by Aurangzeb.

Battle of Alaboi - 10,000 Assamese lost.

UDAYADITYA SINGHA (1670-1674)

Sunyatpha. Battle of Saraighat (1671): Lachit Barphukan sick but fought bravely. Decisive victory for Assamese. Boundary up to Manaha.

POLITICAL INSTABILITY (1673-1681)

Rise of Debera Barbarua. Placed Ramadhvaj Singha, Suhung, Gobar. Killed by Atan Buragohain.

Rise of Atan Buragohain: Placed Sujinpha, Sudaipha.

Rise of Laluk Sola Barphukan: Killed Atan. Surrendered Gauhati to Azamtara (1679). Placed Lora Raja (Sulikpha/Ratnadhvaj). Persecuted princes. Mutilated competitors. Jaymati (wife of Gadapani) tortured at Jerenga pathar. Bhotai assassinated Laluk.

GADADHAR SINGHA (1681-1696)

Supatpha. Battle of Itakhuli (1682) - last battle, Mughals defeated, Manas river remained boundary.

First to introduce land survey and measurement.

Umananda temple (Arjun Daivajna astrologer).

Persecution of Vaishnava Satras (Dakhinpat's Banamali Bapu). False kewaliya bhakats punished.

RUDRA SINGHA (1696-1714)

Sukhrungpha. Abolished beheading before Singarighar.

Synod of Garhgaon (1702) debarred Sudra Mahantas initiating Brahmanas.

Bairagi, Kataky, Khound khels. Bihu in palace. Scholars: Kabiraj Chakravarty.

Khanikar Ghanashyam built Rangpur city.

Wanted to embrace Saktism from Krishnaram Nyayavagish (Parvatiya Gohain).

SIVA SINGHA (1714-1744)

Chatrabhanga phase -> Chief queen Phuleswari (Bar Raja / Pramatheswari).

Forced Maymara Mahanta to take nirmali and blood. Suppressed burying dead.

Phuleswari established tols, scholars (Ruchinath Dvij, Sivada Sarma). Pera-kagaz census. Square coins in Persian.

Ambika (Madanwika) built Shivasagar, Shivadol. Commissioned Hastividyarnava (Dilbar, Dosay, Sukumar Barkath).

Sarbeshwari Bar-Raja.

PRAMATTA SINGHA (1744-1751)

Sunenpha. Rebuilt Rang Ghar.

RAJESWAR SINGHA (1751-1769)

Surempha. Shahjahan of Ahoms (Kareng Ghar, Talatal Ghor, Navagraha, etc.).

Helped Manipuri king Jay Singha (Lata-Kata-Ran). Kuranganayani.

Rise of Kirti Chandra Barbarua. Chakari pheti Buranji destroyed.

LAKSHMI SINGHA (1769-1780)

Sunyeopha. Moamoriya Rebellion started. Kirti Chandra Barbarua killed.

MOAMORIYA REBELLION & MAYAMARA SATRA

GAURINATH SINGHA (1780-1795)

Moamariyas attacked capital. King asked Lord Cornwallis for help.

Captain Welsh arrived 1792, suppressed revolt, left 1795.

Capital shifted to Jorhat.

KAMALESWAR SINGHA (1795-1811)

Purnananda Buragohain de facto ruler.

Dondua Droh (Kamrup) led by Haradatta and Biradatta. Suppressed by Kalia Bhomora (Pratap Ballabh).

Moamorias ceded Matak Kingdom under Sarbananda Singha.

CHANDRAKANTA SINGHA (1811-1817)

Satram conspiracy. Badan Chandra Barphukan fled to Burma.

First Burmese Invasion 1817. Purnananda died of shock. Badan Chandra became Mantri Phukan. Murdered by Rup Singh (Bongal Pukhuri 1819). Ruchinath placed Purandar Singha.

BURMESE INVASIONS & YANDABO

Purandar Singha (1818-1819).
