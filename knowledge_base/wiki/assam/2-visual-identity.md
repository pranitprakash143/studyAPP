2. Visual Identity
The visual look of VisionFlow should feel clean, modern, and high-contrast. It needs to look
trustworthy enough for finance news, but energetic enough for tech and self-improvement.

Mission: To empower curious minds with clear, actionable insights in technology, finance,
and personal growth.
Vision: To become a trusted digital sanctuary where complex information flows into clear
understanding.
Tagline Options:
“Clarity in Motion.”
“Navigate the Future.”
“Insights for the Modern Mind.”
Primary Color: Deep Flow Blue
Hex: #0F4C75
Usage: Backgrounds, Headers, Primary text. Represents trust and intelligence.
Action Color: Electric Teal
Hex: #32E0C4
Usage: Buttons, Links, Highlights, Thumbnail borders. Represents energy and
technology.
