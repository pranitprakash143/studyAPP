CHUTIYA DYNASTY

Birpal was founder (another name Gayapal). Claimed descent from legendary king Bhismak.

Son Gaurinarayan (Ratnadhvajpal) established capital at Ratanpur.

Married Sandhya's daughter to his son Vijaydhvajpal.

Sindhukshetra on Kundil river known as Sadiya.

Last powerful ruler was Dhirnarayan or Dharmadhvajpal.

Dhirnarayan succeeded by son-in-law Nitipal (wife Sadhini).

Annexed to Ahom Kingdom by Suhungmung in 1523 AD and Nitipal was killed.
