5. Social Issues Related to Population
Migration: A major demographic and political issue in Assam is the longstanding history of migration from Bangladesh. This has caused significant social
friction and has been the driving force behind political movements, the Assam
Accord, and the National Register of Citizens (NRC).
Statehood Demands: The diverse population has led to various ethno-linguistic
groups demanding separate statehood or autonomous councils within Assam (e.g.,
Bodoland, Karbiland, Ahomland).
