### 1. Major Regional Kingdoms

* **Kamatapur:** Founded by **Sandhya** after shifting the capital to Kamatapur; **Durlabhnarayan** (1330-50) patronized early poets like Hema Saraswati.
* **Koch Dynasty:** Founded by **Biswa Singha** (1527). **Naranarayan** ("Vikramaditya of Assam") and his general **Chilarai** conquered much of Northeast India and rebuilt the Kamakhya Temple (1565).
* **Chutiya Dynasty:** Founded by Birpal; annexed by the Ahoms in 1523 under Suhungmung.
