### 1. The Varman Dynasty (c. 355 – 650 AD)

* **Pushyavarman:** Founder and contemporary of Samudragupta.
* **Mahendravarman:** First king to perform **Aswamedha yajna**.
* **Bhutivarman:** Issued the **Badaganga epigraph**, the earliest dated inscription in ancient Assam.
* **Bhaskarvarman (c. 594 – 650 AD):**
* Known as **Kumara Raja**; started the Bhaskarabda calendar in 594 AD.
