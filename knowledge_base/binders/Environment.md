# Subject: Environment

## Topic: **1. Population and Demographics Overview * Sources: Assam.pdf ### ## 1. Population and Demographics Overview**
- ## Assam Population Statistics & Demographics (2011 Census & Estimates)

- **Total Population (2011 Census):** 31,205,576
- **India Rank (Population):** 15th
- **Regional Significance:** Most populous state in Northeast India.
- **Population Density:** 465.25 persons/km² (1,205.0/sq mi)
- **Sex Ratio (2025 est.):** 934 females per 1000 males (12th in India)

### Rural vs. Urban Distribution

- Assam is primarily agrarian and rural.
- **Rural Population:** 84.2%
- **Urban Population:** 15.8%

### Official and Spoken Languages

- **Official Language (Statewide):** Assamese
- **Co-Official Language (Statewide):** Bodo (Boro)
- **Additional Official Languages:**
- * Bengali: Official in Barak Valley.
- * Meitei (Manipuri): Recognized in Barak Valley and Hojai district.
- **Scripts:**
- * Assamese & Bengali: Bengali–Assamese script
- * Bodo: Devanagari script
- * Meitei: Meitei script

### Literacy and Human Development

- **Literacy Rate (2024 est.):** 87.0% (Ranked 15th in India)
- **Human Development Index (HDI, 2023):** 0.655
- * **Categorization:** "Medium" human development
- * **India Rank (States & UTs):** 31st

### Social Issues Related to Population

- **Migration:**
- * Longstanding issue involving migration from Bangladesh.
- * Caused social friction.
- * Driving force behind:
- * Political movements
- * Assam Accord
- * National Register of Citizens (NRC)
- **Statehood Demands:**
- * Diverse population leads to demands for separate statehood or autonomous councils.
- * Examples: Bodoland, Karbiland, Ahomland..).
