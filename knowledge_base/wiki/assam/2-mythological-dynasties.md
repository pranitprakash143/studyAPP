### 2. Mythological Dynasties

* **Danava Dynasty:** **Mahiranga Danava** was the earliest known king (a Kirata chief).
* **Asura Dynasty (Narakasura):** Born of Vishnu and Prithvi; he built the **Kamakhya Temple** on Nilachala Hill.
* **Bhagadatta:** Son of Naraka; fought for the Kauravas in the Mahabharata with his elephant **Supratika**.
* **Banasura:** Ruled from Sonitpur; associated with the "Hari-Harar Juddha" and building the Mahabhairav Temple.
* **Bhishmaka:** Ruled Vidarbha (Sadiya); father of **Rukmini**, who was carried off by Lord Krishna.
