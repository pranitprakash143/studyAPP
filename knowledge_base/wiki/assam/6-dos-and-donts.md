6. Do’s and Don’ts
Branding: A subtle consistent border or color splash (Electric Teal) so fans recognize it
instantly.
Video Structure:
The Hook (0:00-0:45): What is the problem?
The Intro: 5-second animated logo sting (VisionFlow).
The Meat: Actionable points.
The CTA: "If you found clarity in this video, hit like..."
Layout:
Use plenty of white space.
Break text with bold key takeaways every 3 paragraphs.
Embed the related YouTube video at the top of the blog post.
SEO Strategy:
Focus on "How-to" and "What is" questions in titles.
Include a "Key Takeaways" summary box at the start of every article.
DO verify all news sources before publishing. Credibility is your currency.
DO cross-pollinate. Mention your blog in videos and embed videos in your blog.
DON’T use clickbait titles that you can't deliver on (e.g., "This stock will 1000x").
DON’T mix politics with news unless it directly impacts technology or finance markets (keep
it objective).
