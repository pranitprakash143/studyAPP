## Summary
The Quit India Movement began after the failure of the Cripps Mission in 1942, with the Indian National Congress adopting the 'Quit India' Resolution on August 8, 1942, and Gandhi giving the 'Do or Die' slogan. This led to the immediate arrest of key Congress leaders and widespread defiance, including sabotage activities, particularly in Assam.

## Key Facts
- The Cripps Mission failed in 1942.
- The Indian National Congress (INC) adopted the 'Quit India' Resolution on 8th August 1942.
- Gandhi gave the slogan 'Do or Die'.
- All important Congress leaders were arrested within a week of the resolution's declaration.
- Gopinath Bordoloi and Siddhi Nath Sarma were arrested at Dhubri.
- Pitambar Dev Goswami and Krishna Dev Goswami were detained.
- The people of Assam defied non-violence by engaging in sabotage activities.
- Kanaklata Baruah led an unarmed procession to hoist the national flag at Gohpur police station.
- Kanaklata Baruah was shot dead while holding the flag during the Gohpur incident.
- Kanaklata Baruah is also known as 'Birbala'.
- Mukunda Kakati picked up the flag after Kanaklata's death but was also killed later in the Gohpur incident.
