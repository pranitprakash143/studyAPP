ART AND ARCHITECTURE

Dah-Parvatiya: Located in the village of Dah Parbatia near Tezpur. One of the oldest Hindu temples in Assam. Early Gupta School of sculpture. Door jambs ornamented with Ganga and Yamuna.

Deopahar / Deopani: Deopani School of Art (late 7th - 8th century AD) in Golaghat. Last phase of Gupta classical style. Transparent drapery, limited jewellery, black sand stone, round face, broad and pointed nose.

Akshaiganga: Remains of 3 temples. Pillar shafts show design resembling Chalukyan style.

Gosaijuri: Stone pieces from Gosaijuri in Nogaon are similar to the art of southern India and Sri Lanka.

Madan Kamdev: Sculptural representations of dance and erotic postures very similar to Khajuraho.
