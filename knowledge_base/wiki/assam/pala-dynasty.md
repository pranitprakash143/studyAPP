PALA DYNASTY

BRAHMAPALA (c. 990-1010 AD)

First elected ruler of Kamarupa.

RATNAPALA (c. 1010 - 1040 AD)

Beautified and well fortified the city of Hadapyaka and renamed it as 'Durjaya' or the 'impregnable one'.

INDRAPALA (c. 1040 - 1065 AD)

Marriage alliance with the Rashtrakutas (Rajyadevi).

Believed to use thirty two Birudas (personal titles).

GOPALA (c. 1065 - 1085 AD)

His mother was a Rashtrakuta princess.

HARSHAPALA (c. 1085 - 1095 AD)

DHARMAPALA (c. 1095 - 1120 AD)

Kalika Puran was written under his patronage.

Shifted his capital city to Kamarupanagar (North Guwahati).

JAYAPALA (c. 1120 - 1038 AD)

Defeated by king Ramapala of Bengal.

Ramapala did not occupy Karaupa proper, only snatched away her possessions in North Bengal and placed this territory under Tingyadeva.

Vaidyadeva succeeded Tingyadeva.

First Gauda ruler to conquer Kamarupa.

Vaidyadeva established the first Brahmana dynasty in Kamarupa.
