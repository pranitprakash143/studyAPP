KAMATAPUR KINGDOM

Rulers: Pratapdhvaj -> Durlabhnarayan (Son) / Dharmanarayan (Cousin).

Other lineage: Sandhya -> Sindhu Rai -> Rup Narayan -> Singhadhvaj. Singhadhvaj killed by minister Pratapdhvaj.

Treaty signed: Dharmanarayan sent 14 families of Brahmanas and Kayasthas to Durlabhnarayan's kingdom, with Kayastha Chandivar (Sankardeva's ancestor) as leader.

Durlabhnarayan (1330-50) gave Chandivar title Devidasa and lands at Bordowa.

Great Poets at the court of Durlabh Narayan

Hema Saraswati: Prahlad Charit, Hara-Gauri-Sambad.

Kaviratna Saraswati: Jayadratha Vadha.

Harihar Vipra: Babrubahanar Yudha, Lava-Kushar Yudha, Tamradwajar Yudha.

Sikandar Shah (1362)

Sultan Sikandar Shah led expedition to Assam. Returned to Bengal as Firoz Shah invaded Bengal.

Other Rulers: Indranarayan (Pancha Gaudeswar), Arimatta, Jongal Balahu.
