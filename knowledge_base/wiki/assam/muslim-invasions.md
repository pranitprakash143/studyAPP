MUSLIM INVASIONS

Mohammad bin Bakhtiyar Khilji (1205-06)

Account found in Tabaquat-i-Nasiri by Minhaj Uddin Siraj.

Ali Mech (first Assamese to get converted into Islam).

His army was defeated by Prithu or Viswasundaradeva (Jalpeswar in Yogini Tantra).

Bakhtiyar was killed by his own minister Ali Mardan.

Kanai Barasi Rock of North Gauhati records (in Sanskrit): 'On the 13th of Chaitra, in the Saka year 1127, the Turks coming into Kamarupa were destroyed'.

Ghiyasuddin Iwaz Khilji (1227)

Governor of Bengal under Delhi Sultan Iltutmish.

Led expedition to Kamarupa in 1227 AD.

Nasiruddin invaded Bengal. Iwaz Khilji was killed by Nasiruddin.

Nasiruddin killed Prithu and placed Sandhya on throne on condition of paying tribute.

Tughril Khan (1257)

Sandhya occupied Gauda and assumed title Gaudeswara.

Tughril Khan marched against him in 1257 AD, but Sultan was killed.

Sandhya shifted capital from Kamarupanagar to Kamatapur. Assumed title Kamateswara or Kameswara.

Muhammad Shah (1337)

Sent 100,000 horsemen to Assam, but the whole army perished in the witchcraft and not a trace was left. (Mayong-India's Black Magic Capital)
