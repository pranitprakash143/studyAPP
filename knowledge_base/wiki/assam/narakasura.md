NARAKASURA

Son of Mother Earth, Prithvi and boar incarnation of Vishnu.

called Bhauma (born of earth).

brought up by king Janaka of Videha.

earliest Aryans in Pragjyotisha.

Built a temple for Kamakhya on the Nilachala hill, excavated a tank and constructed a road to the temple in a single night.

Lord Krishna killed him and placed his son Bhagadatta on the throne.

According to the Vishnu Purana the general of Narakasura was Muru.
