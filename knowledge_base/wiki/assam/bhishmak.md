BHISHMAK

His kingdom is called Vidarbha (may be identified with Sadiya).

His capital was at Kundina, situated on the bank of the river Kundil.

Daughter Rukmini wanted to marry Krishna. However, Bhismaka wanted her to marry Sisupala.

On the wedding day, Krishna carried her off in his chariot to Dwaraka.

The Chutiyas trace their descent from Bhishmaka.
