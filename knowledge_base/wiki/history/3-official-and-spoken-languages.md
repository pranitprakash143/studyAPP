3. Official and Spoken Languages
Assam's linguistic landscape is diverse and constitutionally protected across different
regions.
Official Language (Statewide): Assamese.
Co-Official Language (Statewide): Bodo (or Boro).
Additional Official Languages:
Bengali: Official in the Barak Valley.
